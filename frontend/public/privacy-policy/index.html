Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HLaS (the application used by Cambridge Trout Club and affiliated clubs) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web and mobile services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and club membership details.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, such as photos and preferences.
  • Fishing and activity records you submit through the application (for example beat usage and catch returns).
  • Technical information such as device type, app version, and basic diagnostic logs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain member services and app functionality.
  • To manage memberships, permissions, and club operations.
  • To communicate service updates, support responses, and important notices.
  • To improve reliability, security, and performance of the app.

3. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by applicable law, we process personal data on one or more of these bases: your consent, performance of a contract (membership services), legal obligations, or legitimate interests in running and securing the service.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only:

  • Within your club administration where necessary for club management.
  • With service providers that host or support the app, under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for operational, legal, and record-keeping purposes. Retention periods may vary by data type and club policy.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal data, and to request data portability.

8. Children’s Privacy

The application is intended for club members and administrators and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. International Transfers

If data is processed outside your country, we apply appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: admin@cambridgetroutclub.org
